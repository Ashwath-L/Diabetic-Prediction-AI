Understanding Predictions

The models predict whether a patient belongs to the diabetic or non-diabetic class based on mathematical patterns extracted from historical data.

  • Logistic Regression and ANN calculate numerical output probabilities based on sigmoid transformation.
  • KNN identifies neighbors in feature space to establish a class vote.
  • Decision Trees walk down structural splits to assign the leaf outcome.
