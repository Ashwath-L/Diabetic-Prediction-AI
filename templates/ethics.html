AI Ethics & Responsible AI

Theoretical and practical framework for deploying machine learning systems in healthcare

Educational Disclaimer: This project is developed for Artificial Intelligence internship learning and demonstration purposes only. The prediction generated by this machine learning model is not a medical diagnosis and should not be used for medical decision-making. Please consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical advice.

1. Bias in AI

Machine learning models learn patterns from historical datasets. If the training data contains biases (e.g., underrepresenting certain ethnicities, ages, or genders), the trained model will reflect and amplify these biases in its predictions. For instance, the Pima Indians dataset represents a specific demographic group (female patients of Pima Indian heritage). Applying this model to other demographics could result in poor accuracy due to selection bias.

2. Fairness

Fairness in AI means ensuring that model predictions are equitable and do not discriminate against individuals or groups based on protected attributes like age or race. In clinical settings, fairness requires checking if the false negative rate is high for specific groups (e.g. older patients), which could lead to inequality in healthcare access.

3. Transparency

Transparency involves understanding how an AI makes decisions. Logistic Regression (coefficients) and Decision Trees (decision nodes) are highly transparent, white-box models because their logic is inspectable. In contrast, Artificial Neural Networks (ANNs) are complex black-box models, where mathematical interactions across layers are difficult to explain directly.

4. Privacy Concerns

Clinical metrics are highly sensitive personal information. Developing healthcare AI systems requires adhering to data privacy standards (e.g., HIPAA, GDPR) to ensure that patient records are anonymized, stored securely, and not transmitted to unauthorized third parties.

Ethical AI Practices

In healthcare, AI should act as a Clinical Decision Support System (CDSS), meaning:

  • 🧑‍⚕️ Human-in-the-Loop: The AI assists, but a qualified clinician makes the final medical diagnostic and treatment decisions.
  • 📊 No False Security: A negative prediction from the model should never prevent a patient from seeking professional checkups if they feel unwell.
  • 🧪 Validation: Algorithms must be validated on diverse populations before clinical use.
